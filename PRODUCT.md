# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

BarbaroTech serves small-business owners, founders, and operators who are evaluating a professional website, e-commerce storefront, or custom web application. They need to understand what Anthony can build, see credible examples of shipped work, compare clear starting prices, and contact him without navigating a traditional agency sales process.

## Product Purpose

The site is both Anthony Barbaro's personal portfolio and the client-facing home of his solo website studio. It should turn relevant visitors into qualified conversations by making his capabilities, work, process, pricing, and direct contact options easy to evaluate.

Success means a visitor can quickly decide whether Anthony is a fit, choose a sensible starting package or project path, and request a quote or contact him directly.

## Positioning

BarbaroTech offers direct, one-to-one access to the person who designs and builds the work. Anthony delivers custom, performance-conscious websites and web applications without presenting the business as a staffed agency or relying on page-builder limitations.

## Operating Context

Visitors primarily evaluate BarbaroTech through marketing pages, package pricing, real project case studies, and direct quote or contact paths. Many are likely comparing Anthony with freelancers, small agencies, page-builder services, or do-it-yourself platforms. The experience must work especially well on mobile and make costs, scope boundaries, ongoing care, and next steps understandable before a conversation.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- BarbaroTech is Anthony Barbaro's personal brand and solo website studio.
- BarbaroTech is not currently an LLC and must not be described as an incorporated company.
- Do not imply a team, employees, departments, or agency scale that does not exist.
- Core services include marketing websites, e-commerce storefronts, SEO-focused builds, and custom full-stack or automation work.
- Website packages, setup fees, monthly care plans, add-ons, payment terms, and quote links are maintained in `src/data/pricing.ts`.
- The current entry offer is a limited-time, template-based Starter Website with up to three pages at $399 setup and required $80/month care.
- Public pricing stays simple: one starting package each for a starter website, restaurant website, booking website, online store, and premium website. Custom software is quoted separately.
- Required monthly care is the ongoing service relationship and must be displayed beside every setup price. Extra pages, content, and integrations are added to the quote only when needed instead of becoming more public tiers.
- The public site should remain compatible with static-friendly deployment. Direct phone and email fallbacks must remain available when server-backed forms are disabled.
- Preserve truthful pricing qualifications: third-party services and usage costs remain separate unless explicitly included in a quote.

## Brand Commitments

- Use the name **BarbaroTech** and identify Anthony Barbaro as the individual behind the work.
- The voice should be direct, technically credible, practical, and personal.
- Prefer first-person or clearly solo-studio language over corporate plural language.
- Do not append legal suffixes such as LLC, Inc., or Corp.
- Preserve the real phone number, email address, LinkedIn profile, headshot, project imagery, and existing brand domain already defined in the repository.

## Product Principles

1. Make the solo relationship an advantage: visitors deal directly with the person doing the work.
2. Earn trust through specific work, transparent scope, and honest constraints rather than unsupported marketing claims.
3. Help visitors self-qualify quickly through clear packages, pricing, care options, and next steps.
4. Demonstrate technical depth without making non-technical buyers decode jargon.
5. Keep every primary path fast, responsive, and usable without depending on a functioning contact form.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The public marketing experience must remain keyboard-operable, responsive at mobile through desktop sizes, compatible with reduced-motion preferences, and readable with clear focus states and sufficient contrast.
