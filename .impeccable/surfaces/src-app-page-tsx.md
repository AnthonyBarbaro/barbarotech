# Homepage Portfolio Surface

## Audience and action

Small-business owners and operators should be able to understand the offer, inspect shipped work, choose the closest build type, and request a free custom demo directly from Anthony.

## Chosen direction

Signal Interface: a simple navy, ice-blue, cobalt, and white system. Dark mode remains the default. The existing marketing hero leads directly into clickable screenshots of all hosted portfolio sites, replacing the logo belt, extra wayfinding row, duplicate featured-work gallery, and website-type cards. A concise capabilities section follows. A cobalt Shopify feature and the blog connect visitors to the new service and reading surfaces.

## Content constraints

Use only user-supplied offers and verified project identities. Do not invent testimonials, results, credentials, company scale, or performance claims. Avoid decorative list numbering. Prices and offer terms must match pricing data.

The live collection uses final domains and verified hosted projects. Sour Chamoy Bites and Rancho Bernardo Towing join PartnerSwipe and Wagyu as featured work. Future reusable Shopify themes remain clearly planned, with no fake purchase or download controls.

## Interaction constraints

Hero slides advance when the timeline light completes its pass, support desktop arrows, topic selection, touch swipe, pause, and reduced motion. Website previews stay still, with native horizontal scrolling on phones and a responsive grid on larger screens. Pricing packages use a touch-first scroll-snap deck on mobile and a grid on larger screens. The theme control persists the visitor choice while defaulting new visitors to dark. All project surfaces remain real links.

## Memorable moment

The opening offer changes with the light sweep while real website previews remain anchored directly below it, making the work immediately visible and clickable.
