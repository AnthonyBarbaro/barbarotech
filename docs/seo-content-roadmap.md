# BarbaroTech SEO Content Roadmap

This roadmap supports 100 useful, distinct pages without mass-publishing doorway pages. The first
13 service pages are implemented in `src/data/services.ts`, and the first 10 buyer guides are
implemented in `src/data/guides.ts`. Planned guides stay out of the route tree and sitemap until
they pass the publication gate below.

## Publication gate

A planned page becomes indexable only when it has:

- A buyer question or project decision not already answered by another page.
- First-hand explanation, shipped-project evidence, or a useful original artifact such as a
  checklist, worksheet, comparison framework, or annotated example.
- A unique title, description, heading, canonical URL, and internal-link role.
- Accurate claims that do not imply awards, rankings, testimonials, staff, or results without proof.
- A clear next step that is useful even if the visitor never contacts BarbaroTech.
- A review for overlap with existing pages; merge the topic when a separate URL would add no value.

## Release sequence

Publish in small clusters. After each cluster, review Search Console impressions, query overlap,
indexing, engagement, and qualified inquiries. Expand the pages that demonstrate real demand and
merge or retire pages that do not earn a distinct role.
